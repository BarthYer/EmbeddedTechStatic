Zephyr BLE peripheral

Mar 2, 2025 • Zephyr

Build and test a BLE peripheral with Zephyr RTOS.

west init -m https://github.com/zephyrproject-rtos/zephyr zephyrproject
cd zephyrproject && west update
west build -b nrf52840dk_nrf52840 samples/bluetooth/peripheral
